KNOW WHAT YOU’RE COMPARING

The rules of this experiment

All three strategies get the same starting cash and new savings. Nothing borrows money or sees future prices.

Download monthly results ↓

Lump sum

Invest all starting cash immediately.

DCA

Invest equal portions of starting cash at the beginning of each month during the deployment window.

Weighted DCA

Multiply the regular purchase by 1 + sensitivity × decline from the highest total-return index level observed so far. A 20% dip at sensitivity 5 means a 2× purchase. Purchases are capped by available cash; any remaining cash is invested at the final scheduled purchase.

A fair cash comparison

Uninvested cash earns the selected yield. New savings are invested immediately by all three strategies, starting in month 2; there is no deposit at the ending valuation. The dip rule applies to starting cash only. After deployment, all three simply hold.

A model, with limits

Random paths use independent normally distributed monthly log returns. They do not capture every real market risk. No taxes, fees, or trading costs. Inflation is constant. “Today’s dollars” discounts every valuation for inflation. Illustrative education, not investment advice or a historical S&P 500 backtest.
