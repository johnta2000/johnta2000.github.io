Make the comparison stronger

A quick photo is enough to start.

Send a menu-board photo or itemized receipt in your coffee-picker chat, with the café branch and the date. A screenshot of an ordering app works too, but will be labeled with that app’s name.

  1. Keep the match visible. Include the drink name, size, hot or iced choice, milk and any paid extras.
  2. Include the price context. For a receipt, keep item prices and separate tax, fees, discounts and tip visible. Cover payment details, names and order codes.
  3. Save the finding, not an assumption. We can add confirmed counter prices alongside direct and DoorDash prices, with their source and date. Unreadable or unmatched entries stay unconfirmed.
