Your data, explained

Daylight privacy notice

Last updated October 3, 2026

Daylight is a personal sleep dashboard operated by John Ta. This notice describes the data used by the current version at john-ta.com/tools/sleep/.

Current access: Daylight is a private personal-use dashboard. Access requires sign-in and either operator approval or a valid group invitation. Each account has its own private sleep history and check-ins. The operator administers the backend and can access stored data for support and maintenance. Group members can see only the WHOOP metrics and history window that each person explicitly chooses to share. Demo mode uses fictional data.

What Daylight collects

  • Sign-in information: your email address and account identifier, used to verify access through Clerk.
  • WHOOP data, when you authorize it: sleep dates, performance, consistency, duration, efficiency, estimated deep and REM sleep, and start and end times. With the additional permissions, we also import recovery scores, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, daily strain, and daily workout counts and duration. The connection requests sleep, recovery, cycle, workout, and offline access so it can refresh without asking you to sign in each time. Existing sleep-only connections continue to work until you choose to reconnect for these additional categories.
  • Connection credentials: WHOOP authorization tokens and refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the backend. Your WHOOP password is entered on WHOOP’s own sign-in page, not collected by Daylight.
  • Data you enter or import: sleep records from CSV, JSON, or Apple Health exports; manual sleep entries; and optional alertness ratings and notes. Export files are parsed in your browser. The selected sleep fields are sent to the backend only when you confirm an import; the original export file is not uploaded by this importer. Supported import fields can include heart-rate variability and resting heart rate.

How the data is used

Daylight uses these records to display your sleep history, calculate trends, compare overlapping nights across data sources, and relate sleep to your own alertness check-ins. WHOOP syncing can run when you request it and automatically in the background. The integration requests up to one year of WHOOP history. Connecting WHOOP does not activate friend sharing by itself. When creating or joining a group, you choose individual sleep, recovery, and activity metrics for a rolling history window. Shared metrics are used in group charts, averages, and weekly standings, including changes against your own previous period. Adding new metrics does not enable sharing them automatically. Sharing also covers future synced days and future members of that group. No metrics are selected by default. Personal check-in notes, account credentials, and unselected health metrics are not returned in group views.

Services involved

Clerk handles sign-in, Convex runs the backend and stores the dashboard records, and GitHub Pages hosts the website frontend. WHOOP supplies data you authorize. These providers process information needed to deliver their respective services.

The dashboard uses PostHog for limited page, browser, generic interaction, and error metadata. Its configuration disables session recordings and automatic form capture, strips query strings from recorded URLs, and excludes health measurements and check-in notes from analytics events. This privacy page itself does not load analytics. Hosting and service providers may maintain their own operational logs.

Sharing and security

Daylight does not publish your sleep records, sell them, or use them for advertising. Access is limited as described above, together with the service providers needed to operate the app. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to address misuse or a security incident.

Sign-in checks, restricted dashboard access, HTTPS, and encrypted WHOOP tokens help protect your information. No online service can guarantee complete security. The privacy notice is public so WHOOP can display it; your dashboard and sleep records are not public.

Groups and invitations

Group owners can create single-use invitations that expire after seven days, revoke unused invitations, remove members, or close the group. Treat an invitation like a private access link: anyone signed in who receives it can accept it before it is used or expires. The backend stores a hash of the invitation token. Your browser keeps a pending invitation in session storage during sign-in.

You can change your sharing choices, uncheck all metrics to stop sharing, or leave a group. These changes block subsequent group reads; they cannot erase information someone has already viewed or copied. Leaving a group or being removed does not delete your private history or disconnect your WHOOP account. Membership and invitation records may be retained to operate the service.

Retention and disconnecting

Disconnecting WHOOP in Daylight removes the stored connection and stops future syncing through it; Daylight also requests revocation from WHOOP. You can also revoke Daylight’s access through WHOOP. Disconnecting does not automatically delete previously imported sleep records or check-ins. The current dashboard has no automatic record-expiration schedule.

To request a copy, correction, or deletion of stored records, contact johnta2018@gmail.com. There is currently no self-service delete-all control. Your identity may need to be verified before a request is fulfilled. Service-provider backups and required operational records may remain under their retention practices.

Questions and changes

Contact John Ta at johnta2018@gmail.com about Daylight’s data handling. This notice will be updated when the integration or sharing features change.
