How to export your data

On iPhone, open Health → your profile → Export All Health Data. Unzip the export and choose export.xml here. Daylight reads the sleep records; this is a manual import, not automatic Watch syncing.

Apple Health may include other apps and devices. It is not necessarily Apple Watch-only data.
